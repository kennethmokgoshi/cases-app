**Key Features:**
- **Purple Button** → Team Reporting (`/b2b-dashboard/reporting`) - Shows for MANAGERS & System Admins
- **Orange Button** → Advanced Reports (`/b2b-dashboard/reports`) - Shows for MANAGERS & System Admins
- **Cyan Button** → Submit Referral - Shows for everyone

**Conditional Display:**
- Team Reporting: `session?.user?.isManager || session?.user?.isAdmin`
- Advanced Reports: `session?.user?.isManager || session?.user?.isAdmin`
- Submit Referral: Everyone

**User Roles:**
- Regular B2B Users: See only Submit Referral
- Managers (like Lesego@letsatsifinance.co.za): See ALL THREE buttons
- System Admins: See ALL THREE buttons
