DOCUMENT PACKAGE CONTENTS
================================================================================

This package contains six (6) essential court document templates for debt review 
removal applications in South African High Courts:

1. Notice of Motion
2. Founding Affidavit with Annexures
3. Notice of Set Down
4. Notice of Motion in Rescission Application
5. Court Order (Granted)
6. Proof of Service

All documents comply with:
- Uniform Rules of Court
- National Credit Act 34 of 2005 (NCA)
- South African legal formatting standards

================================================================================
DOCUMENT 1: NOTICE OF MOTION (01_Notice_of_Motion.docx)
================================================================================

PURPOSE:
This is the primary application document that initiates the debt review removal 
proceedings. It sets out the relief sought from the court.

WHEN TO USE:
- Standard debt review removal applications
- When client has completed payment obligations
- When client's circumstances have materially changed

KEY SECTIONS:
- Prayer for relief (rescission of debt review order)
- Directives to credit bureaus and credit providers
- Service requirements for respondents
- Costs provisions

HOW TO COMPLETE:
1. Insert case number (obtain from court registry)
2. Fill in applicant's full name and ID number
3. List all credit providers as respondents
4. Complete physical and email addresses for service
5. Date and sign (attorney's details)
6. Customize relief prayers based on specific case circumstances

TIPS:
- Include NCR as a respondent for comprehensive relief
- Specify 7-day timeframe for credit bureau updates
- Ensure all affected credit providers are listed

================================================================================
DOCUMENT 2: FOUNDING AFFIDAVIT (02_Founding_Affidavit.docx)
================================================================================

PURPOSE:
This is the detailed sworn statement supporting the application. It contains all 
factual evidence and legal grounds for the debt review removal.

WHEN TO USE:
Must accompany every Notice of Motion as the primary evidence document.

KEY SECTIONS:
1. Introduction and Locus Standi
2. Identification of Respondents
3. Debt Review History
4. Compliance with Debt Review Order (THREE OPTIONS):
   - Option A: Full Payment Scenario
   - Option B: Substantially Paid Scenario
   - Option C: Changed Circumstances Scenario
5. Current Financial Position
6. Outstanding Debts (if any)
7. Impact of Debt Review Status
8. Legal Framework and Constitutional Considerations
9. Relief Sought
10. Conclusion

HOW TO COMPLETE:
1. Select the appropriate scenario (A, B, or C) based on client situation
2. Delete inapplicable options
3. Complete all personal details (name, ID, address)
4. Insert specific dates and amounts
5. Attach required annexures:
   - Annexure A: Original debt review application (Form 17.1)
   - Annexure B: Original court order
   - Annexure C: PDA statements/proof of payment/income proof
   - Annexure D: Clearance certificates (if applicable)
   - Annexure E: Current income and expense statement
   - Annexure F: Payment arrangements (if applicable)
   - Annexure G: Correspondence (if applicable)

CRITICAL REQUIREMENTS:
- Deponent must sign in presence of Commissioner of Oaths
- All facts must be within personal knowledge or clearly stated as hearsay
- Commissioner must complete certification section with stamp

TIPS FOR DIFFERENT SCENARIOS:

SCENARIO A - FULL PAYMENT (Strongest case):
- Use when client has paid 100% of restructured debt
- Attach PDA final statement and clearance certificates
- Emphasize rehabilitation achieved

SCENARIO B - SUBSTANTIALLY PAID (Good prospects):
- Use when 90%+ paid or minimal balance remaining
- Show payment history demonstrating reliability
- Explain why remaining balance doesn't warrant continued review

SCENARIO C - CHANGED CIRCUMSTANCES (Requires strong evidence):
- Use when income substantially increased
- Attach 3-6 months of recent payslips
- Show ability to manage debt without review protection
- Consider offering to settle remaining balances upfront

================================================================================
DOCUMENT 3: NOTICE OF SET DOWN (03_Notice_of_Set_Down.docx)
================================================================================

PURPOSE:
Formal notification to the court and all parties that the matter is ready for 
hearing and has been scheduled on the court roll.

WHEN TO USE:
After all pleadings have closed (or opposition period has expired) and you're 
ready to schedule the hearing.

TWO VERSIONS:
- Unopposed Matters: When no answering affidavits received
- Opposed Matters: When respondents have filed opposition

HOW TO COMPLETE:
1. Obtain hearing date from court registrar
2. Select appropriate version (opposed/unopposed)
3. List all documents to be used at hearing
4. Complete attorney details and contact information
5. Serve on all parties at least 10 days before hearing

CERTIFICATE OF SERVICE:
Must complete this section proving service on:
- Court registrar
- All respondents or their attorneys
- Include dates and method of service

TIPS:
- File with court registrar immediately after completion
- Keep proof of filing
- For unopposed matters, confirm no opposition filed
- For opposed matters, file heads of argument 5 days before hearing

DOCUMENT 4: NOTICE OF MOTION IN RESCISSION (04_Notice_of_Motion_Rescission.docx)
================================================================================

PURPOSE:
Specialized application for rescission under Rule 42 of the Uniform Rules. More 
comprehensive than standard Notice of Motion, with detailed legal grounds.

WHEN TO USE:
- When original order needs to be formally rescinded
- When technical legal grounds are stronger than simple application
- When dealing with complex procedural history
- When original debt review order has specific issues

LEGAL GROUNDS INCLUDED:
1. Rule 42(1)(a) - Absence from proceedings
2. Rule 42(1)(b) - Error by the court
3. Common law grounds
4. Section 86(7)(c)(ii)(bb) NCA - Variation of order
5. Section 71 NCA - Amendment of previous order

FIVE GROUNDS OPTIONS:
Ground A: Fulfilment of Obligations (most common)
Ground B: Material Change in Circumstances
Ground C: Error in Proceedings
Ground D: Absence from Proceedings
Ground E: Interests of Justice

HOW TO COMPLETE:
1. Select applicable legal basis (can use multiple)
2. Choose relevant grounds (A through E)
3. Complete all requirements for rescission:
   - Reasonable explanation for default (if applicable)
   - Bona fide application
   - Reasonable promptness
   - Prospects of success
   - No material prejudice to respondents
4. If urgent, complete urgency section with specific reasons

TIPS:
- This is more technical than standard Notice of Motion
- Use when you need stronger legal framework
- Particularly useful for cases with procedural complications
- Include explanation for any delays in bringing application

RESCISSION REQUIREMENTS CHECKLIST:
□ Reasonable explanation provided
□ Application made in good faith
□ Application made with reasonable promptness
□ Applicant has prospects of success
□ No material prejudice to respondents

DOCUMENT 5: COURT ORDER GRANTED (05_Court_Order_Granted.docx)
================================================================================

PURPOSE:
Template of the actual court order to be granted. Can be used as a draft order 
submitted with application or as reference for what the final order should contain.

WHEN TO USE:
- Submit as draft order with unopposed applications
- Use as template when judge requests draft order
- Reference for negotiating consent orders
- Template for registrar when preparing final order

KEY PROVISIONS:
1. Rescission of debt review order
2. Credit bureau directives (7-day removal deadline)
3. Credit provider directives
4. NCR directives (14-day compliance)
5. Debt counsellor directives (if applicable)
6. PDA directives (if applicable)
7. Prohibition on future reporting
8. Enforcement and contempt provisions
9. Costs provisions
10. Liberty to apply
11. Immediate operation clause
12. Service requirements

COMPREHENSIVE DIRECTIVES TO:
- All major credit bureaus (TransUnion, Experian, XDS, Compuscan, SAFPS)
- Each credit provider
- National Credit Regulator
- Debt counsellor
- Payment Distribution Agency

COST OPTIONS:
Option A: Costs in favour of applicant
Option B: No order as to costs (most common for unopposed)
Option C: Each party pays own costs
Option D: Costs reserved

HOW TO USE:
1. Complete all blank fields
2. Select appropriate cost option
3. Submit with application as "Draft Order"
4. Ensure registrar uses this template for final order
5. Once granted, immediately serve on all parties

ENFORCEMENT FEATURES:
- Contempt of court provisions for non-compliance
- Liberty to apply for further relief
- Immediate operation (no waiting period)
- Clear timeframes for compliance
- Reporting requirements

TIPS:
- Always submit a draft order with unopposed applications
- Make it as detailed as possible to avoid ambiguity
- Include specific email addresses for credit bureaus
- Request immediate operation to avoid delays
- Include reporting requirements for accountability

DOCUMENT 6: PROOF OF SERVICE (06_Proof_of_Service.docx)
================================================================================

PURPOSE:
Legal proof that all parties have been properly served with court documents as 
required by the Rules of Court.

THREE FORMATS PROVIDED:
Format A: Sheriff's Return of Service (most formal)
Format B: Affidavit of Service (for service by attorney/agent)
Format C: Certificate of Service by Email (simplified)

WHEN TO USE EACH FORMAT:

FORMAT A - SHERIFF'S RETURN:
- Use for formal personal service
- Required for certain respondents who may oppose
- Most reliable proof of service
- Use when service is challenged

FORMAT B - AFFIDAVIT OF SERVICE:
- Use when attorney or staff effects service
- Multiple methods: registered mail, email, fax, hand delivery
- Requires Commissioner of Oaths
- Include proof of delivery as annexures

FORMAT C - EMAIL CERTIFICATE:
- Use for parties who have agreed to email service
- Use for attorneys of record
- Quickest method
- Attach email delivery reports

HOW TO COMPLETE:

FOR SHERIFF SERVICE:
1. Complete Sheriff's details
2. List all documents served
3. Record each party served separately
4. Include method of service (personal/substituted)
5. Date, time, and location of service
6. Identification method
7. Sheriff signs and stamps

FOR ATTORNEY SERVICE:
1. Deponent makes oath before Commissioner
2. List all documents served
3. Specify method for each party
4. Attach proof: tracking numbers, email confirmations, delivery receipts
5. Commissioner certifies

FOR EMAIL SERVICE:
1. List all recipients with email addresses
2. Include delivery status
3. Attach transmission reports
4. Sign and date

PARTIES TO BE SERVED:
✓ Court registrar
✓ All credit providers (respondents)
✓ National Credit Regulator
✓ Debt counsellor (if still involved)
✓ Any other affected parties

SERVICE METHODS ACCEPTABLE:
- Personal service (preferred)
- Service on attorney of record
- Registered mail
- Email (if agreed or attorney of record)
- Substituted service (if authorized by court)

CRITICAL TIMING:
- Notice of Motion: Respondents need 10 days to file notice of intention to oppose
- Notice of Set Down: At least 10 days before hearing
- Court Order: Within 3-5 days of granting (as specified in order)

TIPS:
- Always keep originals and multiple copies
- File proof of service with court before set down
- For email service, print delivery reports immediately
- If service bounces/fails, re-serve via alternative method
- For opposing parties, use sheriff service for certainty

WORKFLOW: HOW TO USE THESE DOCUMENTS TOGETHER
================================================================================

STEP 1: CLIENT ASSESSMENT (Before drafting)
□ Verify client has grounds for removal
□ Obtain PDA statements and payment history
□ Check credit report for current status
□ Determine which scenario applies (A, B, or C)
□ Calculate outstanding balances (if any)
□ Gather all supporting documentation

STEP 2: DOCUMENT PREPARATION
□ Complete Notice of Motion (Document 1)
□ Complete Founding Affidavit (Document 2) - select appropriate scenario
□ Gather and label all Annexures (A through G)
□ Prepare Draft Court Order (Document 5)
□ Have client sign affidavit before Commissioner of Oaths

STEP 3: FILING WITH COURT
□ Submit Notice of Motion to court registry
□ Submit Founding Affidavit with annexures
□ Submit Draft Court Order
□ Pay prescribed filing fees
□ Obtain case number from registrar
□ File all documents with case number added

STEP 4: SERVICE ON PARTIES
□ Serve all documents on respondents (Document 6)
□ Serve NCR
□ Serve debt counsellor (if applicable)
□ Complete Proof of Service for each party
□ File Proof of Service with court

STEP 5: WAIT FOR OPPOSITION PERIOD
□ Wait 10 days for notice of intention to oppose
□ Monitor for any answering affidavits (15 days after notice)
□ If no opposition received → proceed as unopposed

STEP 6: SET DOWN FOR HEARING
□ Contact court registrar for available hearing date
□ Complete Notice of Set Down (Document 3)
□ Serve Notice of Set Down on all parties
□ File Notice of Set Down with court
□ Prepare for hearing (if unopposed, usually no appearance needed)

STEP 7: HEARING AND ORDER
□ Attend hearing (if required)
□ Obtain granted court order
□ Have order signed by judge and stamped by registrar

STEP 8: IMPLEMENTATION
□ Serve court order on all credit bureaus (within 3 days)
□ Serve court order on all credit providers (within 5 days)
□ Serve court order on NCR (within 5 days)
□ Complete Proof of Service for order
□ Monitor compliance (7 days for credit bureaus, 14 days for NCR)
□ Follow up with non-compliant parties
□ Consider contempt proceedings if necessary

STEP 9: VERIFICATION
□ Obtain updated credit report after 30 days
□ Verify debt review flag removed
□ Confirm NCR records updated
□ Provide confirmation to client
□ Close file

SCENARIO-SPECIFIC GUIDANCE
================================================================================

SCENARIO A: FULL PAYMENT (100% PAID)
Success Rate: 95%+
Timeline: 4-8 weeks

Required Evidence:
- Final PDA statement showing R0 balance
- Clearance certificates from all credit providers
- Paid-up letters
- Original court order

Strategy:
- Emphasize completed rehabilitation
- Show full compliance with original order
- Request costs against credit providers if they oppose unreasonably

SCENARIO B: SUBSTANTIALLY PAID (90%+ PAID)
Success Rate: 80-90%
Timeline: 6-10 weeks

Required Evidence:
- PDA statements showing 90%+ payment
- Explanation of remaining balance
- Payment arrangements for balance (if any)
- Consistent payment history

Strategy:
- Show good payment behavior
- Demonstrate financial responsibility
- Argue remaining balance doesn't warrant continued debt review
- Offer to pay remaining balance as condition of order

SCENARIO C: CHANGED CIRCUMSTANCES
Success Rate: 60-75%
Timeline: 8-12 weeks

Required Evidence:
- 3-6 months recent payslips showing increased income
- Updated income/expense statement
- Proof of changed circumstances (new job, inheritance, etc.)
- Current financial assessment showing no longer over-indebted

Strategy:
- Emphasize material improvement in circumstances
- Show surplus income after expenses
- Demonstrate ability to manage existing debt
- Consider offering payment plan for outstanding debts
- Address Section 79 over-indebtedness criteria directly

SCENARIO D: DEBT COUNSELLOR ABANDONMENT
Success Rate: 85%+
Timeline: 6-10 weeks

Required Evidence:
- Correspondence showing attempts to contact debt counsellor
- Proof of no response from debt counsellor
- Payment history (if available)
- Current status of accounts

Strategy:
- Emphasize impossibility of obtaining clearance certificate
- Show good faith attempts to resolve
- Argue denial of economic rights due to counsellor's failure
- Use rescission application format (Document 4)

COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID
================================================================================

1. INCOMPLETE SERVICE
❌ Not serving NCR
❌ Missing credit providers
❌ No proof of service filed
✓ Serve all parties, keep proof, file with court

2. INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE
❌ No PDA statements
❌ Missing clearance certificates
❌ Outdated financial information
✓ Gather comprehensive supporting documents

3. WRONG LEGAL GROUNDS
❌ Using rescission when simple variation appropriate
❌ Not citing relevant NCA sections
❌ Weak legal arguments
✓ Match legal basis to specific facts

4. POOR AFFIDAVIT DRAFTING
❌ Vague statements
❌ Hearsay without proper basis
❌ Incomplete financial disclosure
✓ Specific facts, proper evidence, complete disclosure

5. TIMING ERRORS
❌ Setting down before opposition period expires
❌ Not allowing sufficient service time
❌ Missing hearing dates
✓ Follow Rules of Court strictly

6. INADEQUATE FOLLOW-UP
❌ Not monitoring credit bureau compliance
❌ Failing to verify NCR update
❌ No proof of implementation
✓ Systematic follow-up and verification

================================================================================
RESPONDING TO OPPOSITION
================================================================================

IF CREDIT PROVIDER OPPOSES:

Common Grounds of Opposition:
- Outstanding balance claimed
- Dispute about payment amounts
- Procedural irregularities

Response Strategy:
1. File replying affidavit within 10 days
2. Attach payment records proving position
3. Request court to verify with PDA
4. Consider costs on attorney-client scale for frivolous opposition

IF NCR OPPOSES:

Common Grounds:
- Incomplete documentation
- Procedural non-compliance

Response Strategy:
1. Address specific concerns raised
2. Supplement documentation if needed
3. Usually resolved by providing missing information
