QUICK WORKFLOW (9 STEPS)
================================================================================

1. ASSESS CLIENT
   □ Verify grounds for removal
   □ Gather all evidence documents
   □ Determine scenario (A, B, or C)

2. COMPLETE DOCUMENTS
   □ Fill in Notice of Motion (Doc 1 or 4)
   □ Complete Founding Affidavit (Doc 2) - delete unused scenarios
   □ Attach all annexures (A-G)
   □ Prepare draft order (Doc 5)

3. SIGN & COMMISSION
   □ Client signs affidavit before Commissioner of Oaths
   □ Commissioner completes certification

4. FILE WITH COURT
   □ Submit all documents to court registry
   □ Pay filing fees
   □ Obtain case number

5. SERVE PARTIES (Within 3 days of filing)
   □ All credit providers
   □ National Credit Regulator
   □ Debt counsellor (if applicable)
   □ Complete Proof of Service (Doc 6)

6. WAIT 10 DAYS
   □ Monitor for notice of opposition
   □ If no opposition → proceed to step 7
   □ If opposed → file replying affidavit

7. SET DOWN HEARING
   □ Contact court registrar for date
   □ Complete Notice of Set Down (Doc 3)
   □ Serve all parties

8. ATTEND HEARING
   □ Appear in court (if required)
   □ Obtain granted order

9. IMPLEMENT ORDER
   □ Serve order on all credit bureaus (within 3 days)
   □ Serve order on NCR (within 5 days)
   □ Monitor compliance
   □ Verify removal after 30 days
